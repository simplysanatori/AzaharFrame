# Azahar for Frame artwork: credits

## Azahar logo and wordmark

- The flower and the "Azahar" wordmark are the Azahar Emu Logo, (c) 2024 angyartanddraw and PabloMK7, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- Lockup source: https://azahar-emu.org/resources/images/logo/azahar-name-and-logo.svg (the banner at the top of the azahar-emu/azahar README).
- App icon flower: `dist/azahar.svg` from the Azahar source tree.
- Changes made: the word "Emulator" in the official lockup was replaced with "for Frame". It uses the same typeface, black outline, green-to-cream fill gradient and drop shadow as the original. The line was scaled so it ends where "Azahar" ends. The flower and "Azahar" are unchanged.

## Typeface

- Blooming Grove Bold (version 006.000) by Nathan Eady. This is the font named in Azahar's wordmark SVG (`font-family: BloomingGroveBold`). The font file says: "This font has been released into the public domain by the author."
- Downloaded from https://www.dafont.com/bloominggrove.font, which lists the licence as public domain.
